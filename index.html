Engineered for Accessibility & Efficiency

Overcoming digital literacy hurdles and physical mandi congestion through smart multi-channel technology.

1

Assisted Booking / सहायता डेस्क

Designed for farmers without smartphones. Procurement operators and CSC agents register farmers and book slots in 60 seconds on their behalf.

2

Multi-Channel Access / बहु-माध्यम

A unified relational database links Web Portals, Mandi Helpdesks, Village CSC Centers, and Future IVR voice calls to one consistent record.

3

Smart Slot Management / स्मार्ट स्लॉट

Strict capacity balancing prevents overcrowding. Once hourly capacity is reached, the system smoothly redirects to subsequent windows.

4

Token & Live Queue / टोकन एवं कतार

Sequential, transparent token numbers (e.g. 101, 102...) per centre and date. Operators call next in line with real-time status updates.

5

Simulated SMS Engine / एसएमएस सूचना

Zero-cost mock SMS dispatcher logs confirmations, queue alerts, and delivery receipts in the database with bilingual alerts for farmers.

6

Transparent Procurement & MSP

Real-time auditing from slot booking to weighing, completion, and direct payment tracking under official Minimum Support Price standards.
